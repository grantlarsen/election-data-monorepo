## Gate behavior and validation

`--publication-manifest` is optional and preserves the legacy full-comparison format and fingerprints. With a subset manifest, the expected approval SHA is the subset's `publication_sha256`, not the full vintage diff's `proposal_sha256`. The gate checks every selected predecessor and full proposed payload before any INSERT. Missing approval/actor, stale scope/context/input/head/payload, duplicate/out-of-scope IDs and missing risk flags fail closed. New selected decisions also require explicit approval. The entire publication transaction includes context, reporting units and append-only successors; unselected reporting units are never inserted.

Each selective successor records its actor, context digest and subset digest in its immutable evidence. Replaying the same authorization recognizes only the exact direct successor with matching payload, context, actor and subset audit digest; it inserts zero decisions and zero reporting units. A later head makes the old authorization stale. The existing source-row lock and database chain constraints retain concurrent-writer protection; a conflicting concurrent transaction must be retried with current review state.
